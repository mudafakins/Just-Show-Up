Just Show Up!  -  install notes for teammates
==============================================

Needs: an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 or later.

1. If an older copy is running, click its menu bar icon, open the gear
   menu and choose Quit.

2. Drag "Just Show Up!" into your Applications folder (replace the old one).

3. Open it. macOS will refuse the first time because the app is not from
   the App Store. To allow it:
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down to the message about "Just Show Up!" and click
       "Open Anyway", then confirm.
   You only do this once per version.

4. Look for the two stars in the menu bar (top right of the screen).
   The app has no Dock icon or window.

5. First time only: type your name, choose "Join a Team" and enter the
   team code you were given. If you were already on the team, you are
   still on it and can skip this.

Permissions (macOS asks the first time you use each feature):
  - Microphone: allow it when you make or accept your first call.
  - Screen Recording: needed only to share YOUR screen. Click the share
    button during a call, choose "Allow Screen Recording...", switch the
    app on in System Settings, then quit and reopen the app.

If sharing still asks for permission after you allowed it, switch
"Just Show Up!" off and on again in
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording,
then quit and reopen the app.

How to use it:
  - Click a teammate's name to call them.
  - Hold the round button on the right of their row to talk instantly
    (walkie-talkie). Wait for the short chirp, then speak.
  - During a call, use the share button to share a screen or one window.
  - When a teammate shares, drag in the window that opens to draw on
    their screen.
